Parameter Guide

What each value means

📏
Height & Weight

Used to compute your BMI. Use a measuring tape for height and a weighing scale for weight.

💉
Blood Pressure (ap_hi / ap_lo)

Measure with a BP machine. ap_hi = systolic (upper number), ap_lo = diastolic (lower). Normal: 120/80.

🧪
Cholesterol

From a blood test. 1 = Normal (<200 mg/dL), 2 = Above Normal (200–239), 3 = High (240+).

🍬
Glucose

Fasting blood sugar test. 1 = Normal (<100 mg/dL), 2 = Pre-diabetic, 3 = Diabetic range.

⚖️
BMI (Body Mass Index)

Calculated as weight(kg) ÷ height(m)². Under 18.5 = underweight, 18.5–24.9 = normal, 25+ = overweight.

🚶
Physical Activity

Are you physically active? Yes if you exercise or walk regularly for at least 30 min/day.
