📊 How the charts work

These charts call /api/vizdata?dataset=cardio on your Flask backend. Your dataset.py loads the CSV, computes summaries (counts, averages, distributions), and returns JSON. Chart.js renders it client-side. To add a new chart: add a route in app.py returning aggregated JSON, then add a canvas + fetch call here.
